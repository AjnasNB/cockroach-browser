Observe. Propose. Approve. Execute. Prove.

A browser is powerful because it carries state and can change real systems. Cockroach Browser makes that power explicit at every step without reducing the agent to screenshots and coordinates.

01

Observe

Capture a bounded semantic snapshot with snapshot-scoped refs, source URL, digest, and challenge state.

02

Propose

Name the exact action, target ref, purpose, effect, and expected revision.

03

Approve

When configured, route consequential actions through Maqam for policy and exact one-use approval.

04

Execute

Run the admitted action inside one authorized Chromium session and finite budget.

05

Prove

Keep artifacts and a hash-chained receipt linking input, decision, outcome, and evidence.

One product stack. Four distinct authorities.

The packages interoperate through explicit adapters. They do not silently merge credentials, policies, browser profiles, or ledgers.

Execute

Cockroach Browser

Owns Chromium, sessions, semantic refs, interactions, browser evidence, audits, and local worker transport.

Discover

Cockroach Crawler

Maps and extracts bounded public sources at breadth, then hands selected rendered paths to the browser.

Remember

Qarinah

Stores compact cited read outcomes and source links without gaining browser dispatch or profile authority.

Govern

Maqam

For operations routed through its adapter, owns policy, exact approval, replay protection, dispatch, and governance receipts.

Challenges are a handoff, not a bypass target.

The runtime detects login, consent, CAPTCHA, and access challenges, records the state, pauses automation, and waits for a human or authorized resolver. It does not defeat site controls or claim access after a site denies it.
